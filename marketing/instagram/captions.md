## Day 3 — Carousel: How to work out your food cost %

Save this before your next menu change 📌

Food cost % = ingredient cost per portion ÷ selling price EXCLUDING VAT × 100.

Most owners forget to take the VAT off, which makes every dish look more profitable than it is.
